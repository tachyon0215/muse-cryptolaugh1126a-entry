# muse-cryptolaugh1126a-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Laugh Track: The Crypto Trading Joke Contest](https://make.fun/bounty/u-alfasurya91-laugh-track-the-crypto-trading-joke-a454158a).

## The entry

> My bags are down 90%, the group chat still calls it a dip, and I've stopped buying - at this depth, the dip doesn't need a buy order, it needs a submarine.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: earned trading details (bags down 90%, the group chat still calling it a dip) escalate the word 'dip' past its trading sense, ending on the submarine image - tight, true to trading life, and repeatable.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt`.
